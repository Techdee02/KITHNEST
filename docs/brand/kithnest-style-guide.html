Kithnest

Brand & Style Guide

A parent-first school app for Nigerian nursery and primary schools. This page documents Kithnest's actual production tokens — colors, type, surfaces — pulled directly from the live app's design system, for consistent use anywhere the brand shows up outside the product.

Logomark

Overlapping arcs, cradling a seed

An abstract nest — two woven arcs holding a small seed at the centre. No stock icon. Use the full lockup by default; the mark alone works at small sizes (favicons, avatars, app icons).

Kithnest
Kithnest

Minimum clear space around the mark: half the mark's own height. Never recolor the arcs or seed outside this palette — the teal/marigold/coral relationship is the mark.

Color

Marigold & Deep Teal

Four ramps, ten steps each. 500 is the working shade for each hue; lighter steps are tints and backgrounds, darker steps are text-on-light and hover/active states.

MarigoldPrimary — CTAs, highlights, the wordmark's second syllable
TealSecondary — progress, confirmation, the logomark's outer arc
CoralAccent — alerts, overdue states, the logomark's seed
InkWarm neutrals — text & backgrounds, never cool/blue-grey

Typography

Fraunces & Plus Jakarta Sans

A warm serif for headings and moments of warmth, paired with a clean grotesk for everything read at length or operated as UI.
